# Business Metrics Definitions

## 1. Executive Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **Total Revenue**: Sum of all sales revenue across non-cancelled orders.
  $$\text{Total Revenue} = \sum \text{Sales}$$

- **Total Orders**: Count of distinct order IDs.
  $$\text{Total Orders} = \text{CountDistinct}(\text{Order\_ID})$$

- **Average Order Value (AOV)**: Average revenue generated per order transaction.
  $$\text{AOV} = \frac{\text{Total Revenue}}{\text{Total Orders}}$$

- **Profit Margin %**: Percentage of gross revenue retained as net profit.
  $$\text{Profit Margin \%} = \left( \frac{\text{Total Profit}}{\text{Total Revenue}} \right) \times 100$$

- **Revenue Growth Rate %**: Period-over-period revenue growth comparison.
  $$\text{Growth Rate \%} = \left( \frac{\text{Revenue}_{\text{Current}} - \text{Revenue}_{\text{Previous}}}{\text{Revenue}_{\text{Previous}}} \right) \times 100$$

## 2. RFM Customer Segmentation Matrix

- **Recency (R)**: Days elapsed since customer's most recent purchase date.
- **Frequency (F)**: Total number of distinct orders placed by customer.
- **Monetary (M)**: Total monetary sales spend generated by customer.

| Segment | RFM Scoring Criteria | Actionable Business Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| **Champions** | R=4-5, F=4-5, M=4-5 | Early access to new releases, loyalty rewards |
| **Loyal Customers** | R=3-5, F=3-5, M=3-5 | Upsell higher-tier products, referral incentives |
| **Potential Loyalists**| R=3-5, F=1-3, M=1-3 | Offer membership programs, cross-sell products |
| **At Risk** | R=1-2, F=3-5, M=3-5 | Re-engagement email campaigns, targeted discounts |
| **Lost Customers** | R=1-2, F=1-2, M=1-2 | Win-back surveys, low priority marketing spend |
