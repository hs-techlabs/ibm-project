1. PROJECT OVERVIEW & PURPOSE
-----------------------------
Vanguard is an enterprise-grade Decision Intelligence Platform built upon 533,878
verified transactions across 38 global markets from the UCI Machine Learning
Repository. It bridges the gap between raw relational transactional records and
executive boardroom decisions by operationalizing the complete commercial
analytics continuum:
DATA -> INFORMATION -> INSIGHTS -> DECISION -> ACTION

2. FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS (FR)
-------------------------------
FR-01: Data Ingestion & Sanitization Engine
  - Ingest 533k+ transactions from the UCI Machine Learning Repository.
  - Automatically filter non-retail administrative codes (POST, BANK CHARGES, etc.).
  - Accurately isolate returns (InvoiceNo starting with 'C' or negative Quantity).
  - Reconcile guest accounts (25.3% of transactions) without corrupting customer RFM cohorts.
  - Implement fallback self-healing data pipeline to download/clean if local cache is missing.

FR-02: Executive Decision Cockpit
  - Display 6 C-suite executive KPIs: Net Revenue ($8.87M), Gross Profit ($3.93M),
    Total Orders (22.2k), Average Order Value ($399), Gross Margin (44.3%), and Return Rate (2.45%).
  - Provide period-over-period delta badges and dynamic executive alert banners.

FR-03: Revenue & Sales Velocity Module
  - Render dual-axis monthly revenue versus order volume trends.
  - Render intraday checkout velocity heatmaps (Day of Week vs Hour of Day).

FR-04: Catalog & Category Intelligence
  - Map SKU descriptions into 8 retail departments using keyword taxonomy.
  - Compute cumulative Pareto 80/20 SKU distribution curves.
  - Identify top gross margin drivers and return-vulnerable product lines.

FR-05: Algorithmic RFM Customer Segmentation
  - Score customers across Recency (days), Frequency (orders), and Monetary (spend) quintiles.
  - Classify accounts into actionable cohorts: Champions, Loyalists, Promising New,
    At-Risk, and Hibernating.
  - Provide cohort spend distributions and downloadable CSV segments.

FR-06: Corporate Risk & Opportunity Engine
  - Quantify domestic market over-reliance (UK accounts for 82.4% of topline revenue).
  - Quantify high-value customer churn exposure ($1.15M baseline replenishment at risk).
  - Sizing cross-border wholesale expansion ($485 international AOV vs $228 UK domestic).

FR-07: Predictive Demand & Price Elasticity Simulator
  - Execute Holt-Winters statistical time-series forecasting with 95% confidence intervals.
  - Provide interactive sliders for price adjustment (-20% to +30%) and COGS inflation (-10% to +20%).
  - Simulate revenue, gross profit, and unit volume outcomes under inelastic, unit elastic,
    and elastic curves.

FR-08: Consolidated Single-File Executable Delivery
  - Entire application runs via a self-contained project.py script adhering to IBM submission guidelines.

3. NON-FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS (NFR)
-----------------------------------
NFR-01: Performance & Startup Latency
  - Sub-second deserialization and dashboard boot (< 2 seconds) via Apache PyArrow Snappy Parquet caching.
NFR-02: Cross-Platform Portability
  - Pure Python implementation compatible with macOS, Linux, and Windows 10/11.
NFR-03: Package Size Compliance
  - Total submission archive strictly under 10.0 MB for portal upload constraints.
NFR-04: Resilience & Self-Healing
  - Automatic download and cleaning pipeline if local data files are not present.
NFR-05: Documentation & Attribution Integrity
  - Preservation of open-source MIT License and attribution to foundational architecture.
